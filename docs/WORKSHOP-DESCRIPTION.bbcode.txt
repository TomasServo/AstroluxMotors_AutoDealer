[b]Vehicle appraisal[/b]
Trade-in appraisal supports reviewed vehicle profiles and compatible equipment mappings. Supported installed accessories can appear as separate appraisal values. Eligibility, equipment recognition and valuation depend on the vehicle and its integration; universal support for every vehicle or accessory is not promised.

[h2]BUILT FOR CUSTOM WORLDS[/h2]
[b]Your dealership. Your inventory. Your economy.[/b]

World owners configure the vehicles offered for sale, their prices and where purchases are delivered.

[list]
[*]Configurable vehicle catalogs and sales offers.
[*]Owner-defined vehicle pricing.
[*]Model, variant and available color/design pricing controls.
[*]Dynamic demand pricing or static pricing.
[*]Delivery areas defined using two corners.
[*]Selectable vehicle delivery direction.
[*]Optional banking integration through a documented adapter interface.
[/list]

Use dynamic demand pricing for changing prices, or static pricing for predictable costs.

[b]Optional banking[/b]
A compatible bank bridge must be supplied and configured separately. The adapter interface does not provide a ready-to-use connection to every banking mod.

[b]Native building behavior[/b]
Showroom windows and doors retain native breakable behavior. Astrolux does not bundle a Java protection component. Owners who need protected premises should configure their server's safe-zone system and verify what it protects.

[h2]GETTING STARTED[/h2]
[b]For world owners and administrators[/b]

In a supported test setup, place an Astrolux Motors Sales Kiosk, configure the available vehicles and prices, then define a clear delivery area and direction. Test a purchase and delivery before making the dealership available to other players.

[b]Showroom setup[/b]
Single-player owners can place a furnished tower. In multiplayer, tower placement and deletion require administrator access. Multiplayer remains experimental; back up your world before using placement tools.

The optional single-player demo includes the approved showroom on an 80×80 lot, two configured delivery areas and $25,000 starter cash once per character. Follow the Challenges instructions below. The preserved save is also available as an optional download.

[b]Native furniture visibility[/b]
Couches and tables may become translucent when they obscure the player. This is normal Project Zomboid visibility behavior and is retained in 0.9 beta.

[h2]FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS[/h2]
[b]Do I need a banking mod?[/b]
No. Supported purchases work with carried cash. Bank payments require a separately supplied compatible integration.

[b]Can I customize my purchase?[/b]
You can select available colors and compatible existing designs. Choices vary by vehicle and installed content.

[b]Can owners control prices?[/b]
Yes. Owners configure vehicle offers and pricing, with static or dynamic demand pricing.

[b]Can I trade in my current vehicle?[/b]
Eligible supported vehicles can be appraised through the trade-in interface. Review the appraisal before accepting. Vehicle and accessory support depends on the available profiles and mappings.

[b]Can I make a compatibility harness?[/b]
Yes. Independently written small connection mods are permitted under the harness terms below. They must require the original Astrolux Motors mod and must not bundle or reproduce its code or assets.

[b]Where do purchased vehicles appear?[/b]
In the configured delivery area, facing the selected direction. Keep the area clear and large enough for the vehicle. A purchase awaiting delivery is not a reason to buy it again; check the order's status first.

[b]Can I place the showroom in a fresh world?[/b]
A public setup candidate is prepared, but its live acceptance is pending. Confirm that your test package includes it and follow its matching setup guide. The candidate supports one active showroom per world; placement checks for a clear site and requires confirmation.

[b]Is multiplayer verified?[/b]
Multiplayer is experimental in 0.9 Beta. Multiplayer testers are welcome; please use a dedicated test server rather than an established world.

[h2]BUG REPORTS & FEEDBACK[/h2]
[b]Single-player and multiplayer mod testers wanted![/b]

Help test Astrolux Motors in single-player worlds and multiplayer servers. Feedback is especially welcome on purchases, vehicle delivery, trade-ins, pricing, showroom interaction, save/reload behavior and compatibility with other vehicle mods.

For multiplayer testing, please also check admin-only placement, ordinary-player permission limits, simultaneous purchases, vehicle/key ownership, late joining, reconnects and server restarts. Use a fresh dedicated test server with matching mod files on every client. Multiplayer is experimental; the tester call does not imply established multiplayer support.

Astrolux Motors is under active development and testing. Detailed reports help identify problems and improve the dealership experience.

When reporting a problem, please include:

[list]
[*]Your Project Zomboid build and Astrolux version.
[*]Whether the issue occurred in single-player or multiplayer.
[*]Relevant vehicle mods and optional integrations.
[*]Steps to reproduce the issue, including what you expected and what happened.
[*]The relevant console error, if one appeared.
[*]Screenshots of the kiosk, order status or delivery area where useful.
[/list]

For a purchase problem, include whether cash was deducted, whether a vehicle appeared and whether you received its key. Remove private information from logs before sharing them.

[h2]ASTROLUX MOTORS AUTO DEALER - SINGLE-PLAYER CHALLENGE[/h2]
Explore the furnished Astrolux showroom on its 80 x 80 demonstration lot. Start a new Challenge or import the current bundled saved world.

[b]Automatic Challenge[/b]
[list]
[*]Enable [b]Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer[/b] and [b]Astrolux Test Lot (Single Player Preview)[/b]. Both are required for this Challenge. Allow the game to reload its Lua files or restart when prompted.
[*]Open [b]New Game -> Choose Mods[/b], confirm both Astrolux mods are selected for the new world, click [b]Accept[/b] and allow Lua to reload.
[*]Choose [b]Challenges -> Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer[/b] and create your character.
[*]The showroom is placed automatically once when the area loads. No ZIP extraction, debug mode, manual paste or external installer is required.
[*]Quit normally to save. Use [b]Load[/b] to continue; starting the Challenge again creates a different world.
[/list]
Reloading preserves your world and does not create a duplicate showroom.

[b]Optional bundled saved world[/b]
[url=https://github.com/TomasServo/AstroluxMotors_AutoDealer/raw/refs/tags/v0.9-beta/bundled-saves/Astrolux%20Motors%20Auto%20Dealer.zip]Download Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer.zip[/url]
Use [b]Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer.zip[/b] from this release's bundled-saves directory. This is the current working world tested with the accompanying mods, rather than the older frozen prototype. Both matching mods are required; the save ZIP alone does not install them.

Exit the game, extract the save ZIP to a temporary folder, and copy its Saves directory into your game profile. The final folder structure must be:
[code]Saves/Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer/Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer/<save files>[/code]
On a standard Windows installation this is inside [b]C:\Users\YOUR-NAME\Zomboid[/b]. Use your configured profile directory if you launch with a custom cachedir.

Keep both named directory levels. Do not move the world to Sandbox, add an extra nested Saves directory, merge worlds or overwrite an existing save. Back up an existing same-name world outside the Saves directory before importing. Enable both matching mods and choose [b]Load -> Astrolux Motors Auto Dealer[/b]. Avoid duplicate local and Workshop installations of the same mods.
